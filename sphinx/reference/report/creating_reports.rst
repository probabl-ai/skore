Creating reports
================

.. currentmodule:: skore

:func:`evaluate` and :func:`compare` are the entry points for building reports.
:func:`evaluate` builds an estimator, cross-validation, or comparison report from
estimators and data. :func:`compare` consolidates existing reports into a comparison
report.
