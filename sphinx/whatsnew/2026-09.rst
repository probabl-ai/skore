What's new in skore — September 2026
====================================

Paginated metrics summaries
---------------------------

HTML representations of metric summaries now show 10 rows per page, so
multi-output and multiclass tables stay readable. :meth:`~skore.MetricsSummaryDisplay.frame`
is unchanged and still returns every row. See :issue:`3113` by :user:`GaetandeCast`.
